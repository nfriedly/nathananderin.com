Keep Moving: Notes on Loss, Creativity, and Change Book Review

by Erin Friedly

Keep Moving: Notes on Loss, Creativity, and Change

Goodreads Amazon Open Library

Reviewed on: 2025-10-27

I would recommend this for almost anyone going through difficulty which feels like all of us these days. It is a book full of how one creative writer got through her own difficult divorce and difficulties like the transition into motherhood and the miscarriages that preceded the divorce.

It is an entire book of how one gets to the place where you want to do the next right thing again and again and again.
