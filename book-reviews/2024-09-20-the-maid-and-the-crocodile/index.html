The Maid and the Crocodile Book Review

by Erin Friedly

The Maid and the Crocodile

Goodreads Amazon Open Library

Reviewed on: 2024-09-20

The world building, the prose, it is all so, so good. Don’t sleep on this read. Read the Raybearer and Redemptor and then read this one. I will read everything Jordan Ifueko writes forever.

Also the audiobook version only serves to level up what is already a 5 ⭐️ read.
