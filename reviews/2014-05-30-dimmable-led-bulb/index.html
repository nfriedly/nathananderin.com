Flickers slightly when dimmed

Lighting EVER GU10 LED Bulb (4W) Review

by Nathan Friedly

Lighting EVER Dimmable 4W GU10 LED Bulbs, 35W Equivalent, Recessed Lighting, Track Lighting, Warm White

Amazon

Original URL: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B007V1VO06

Reviewed on: 2014-05-30

This is the first dimmable LED I've tried. It works great at full brightness, but the hood that I installed it in has a lower 2 levels of brightness, and at the lower level this bulb flickers just enough to be annoying. It reminded me of an old CRT monitor running at 60 hertz. (I guess a lot of people aren't bothered by that so maybe my eyes are just more sensitive than most...)
