These are super cute little butterfly hair clips!

They're smaller than I was expecting at a touch over a half inch / 15mm, but they're cute and colorful and easy to work with. There's enough of them that we don't have to worry about potentially loosing one or running out.

The case's snap doesn't feel the most sturdy, but it should be fine for storing them in a drawer or closet or similar.
