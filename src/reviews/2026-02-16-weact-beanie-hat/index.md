I wore this hat for a walk through our local nature preserve, and thoroughly enjoyed it. The hat is warm and cozy and comfortable, just the right amount of tightness so that it held on to my head but didn't squeeze so hard that my glasses hurt.

This was a day when it got above freezing and things were just starting to melt. The hat felt relatively breathable, and by the end of the walk I did take it off for a few minutes to cool down. It compresses down, pretty small to be able to squeeze into a pocket when I'm not wearing it.

I'm also a fan of the teal blue color, I think it looks nice, but I also think it's a color that stands out without screaming "high visibility".

All told, I'm very happy with this hat.
