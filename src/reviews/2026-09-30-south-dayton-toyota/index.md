On the positive side, they were friendly, accommodating, and not very pushy. Probably the best experience I've ever had purchasing from a dealership. Randy in particular was very easy to work with. The core functionality of the vehicle seems to be in good shape - it drives well, the AC works, etc.

On the negative side, the "Toyota Certified Used Gold" doesn't seem to be as good as I'd expected: a number of details got missed like a broken sun visor, a stuck seat, a missing key, a broken speaker, and a couple other small things along those lines. The dealership handled most of it already and is waiting on a part for the speaker, but I thought the certification meant this stuff would have been caught and corrected beforehand. Since purchasing it a week or two ago, I think I've spent more time with a loaner than my vehicle.
