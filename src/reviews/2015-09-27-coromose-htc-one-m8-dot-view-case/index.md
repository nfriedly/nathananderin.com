Works perfectly, and a much better deal than the official one. I seem to drop my phone a lot, so I've already cracked two of these cases (on my third one now), but so far my phone has escaped unscathed.

The flap can be a bit annoying when trying to do a lot of reading, but just fold it behind the case. After a week or two of this, it gets more "broken in" and can be held back more easily (but it still has enough spring to snap shut before the phone hits the ground if you drop it.)
