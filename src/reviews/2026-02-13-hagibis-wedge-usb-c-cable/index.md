Works great for charging and low-speed data (USB 2.0: 30-40MB/s real-world).

This is a handy little cable that I plan to use for connecting things like my phone or earbuds to my laptop for charging or moving a few files over. The orange color of mine will make it easy to find in my bag.

I got the 0.5 foot version, which is really more like 4 inches + connectors, so I'm probably not going to ever use it at the full 240W, but I confirmed that does have the correct e-marker chip enabling that level of charging and it's low resistance of 47mΩ means that charging losses would be in the ballpark of 1% (possibly less since I had to bend the cable pretty sharply to even connect it to my tester).

The slight wedge shape of the connector makes it easy to get a grip on while still being small enough to fit through the hold my phone case. (The housing measures 6.0x10.9mm on the connector end, expanding to 7.7x10.9mm on the cable end. It's 18.8mm long.)

There's a modest 6.6mm of strain relief, but I wouldn't expect much more from a cable of this length.

The braided cable looks and feels nice and is fairly flexible.

It's labeled with it's 240W charging speed on one side of each connector, but the other side just has it's "HAGiBiS" branding. It doesn't have the data speed anywhere.

Overall, it's a good cable that does what it says.
