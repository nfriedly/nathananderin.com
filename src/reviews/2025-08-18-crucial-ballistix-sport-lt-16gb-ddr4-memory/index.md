Good RAM, served me well for years. A single 2x8gb kit works great at the advertised XMP speeds, but 2 kits (4 sticks) had to drop down to slower JDEC speeds. Might have just been the motherboard, though (Asus B350-F Gaming).

The lenovo motherboard & bios in *this* build is locked down and won't go above 2400mhz, so it's kind of wasted potential here. The other pair is in my file server now, I may swap in some slower RAM here and put this someplace where the speed can actually be used.
