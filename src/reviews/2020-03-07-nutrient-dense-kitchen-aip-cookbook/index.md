I’ve had this book for a little over a year now, and I’ve cooked from it quite a lot. As a former fan of cream of chicken soup from the can the Cream of Chicken Soup with Broccoli and Root Vegetables is the perfect comfort food when I can find celeriac root at the store. Whether you have food allergies or are in the elimination phase of the AIP these recipes are flavorful and won't leave you feeling like you’ve lost all the foods and flavors you love.
