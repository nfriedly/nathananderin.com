Works great, an excellent way of adding a bit more performance to a basic office PC. Having two SATA connectors means it isn't exceeding the 54W (4.5A @ 12V) of the SATA specification if the GPU draws the full 75W (6.25A @ 12V) that the PCIe 6-pin specification allows. (Although I went with a GPU that only drew about 40W to leave a bit of extra margin for safety.)
