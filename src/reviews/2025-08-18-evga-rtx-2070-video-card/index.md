Great GPU, just wildly overpriced. I picked it up for just over $300 after the 20-super series launched and, honestly, that should have been the launch MSRP.  It's literally the most I've ever spent on a GPU (and quite possibly the most I ever will.)

I do like that it has a full-featured USB-C port - I think they intended it for some VR nonsense, but it was just nice to have in general. When I got it, it was the both the fastest USB port and the only Type-C port in my system!

My first one died on me a few months after I got it, but EVGA replaced it under warranty and the replacement has been going strong for years.
