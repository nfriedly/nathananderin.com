I bought a 3-pack, but only two of them worked. They gave me a 1/3 refund, so I guess it's fine. My motherboard doesn't have the right connections for the RGB, it only has the 12v 4-pin, whereas this one needs the 5v 3-pin. Oh well, they still keep the system cool. I may swap them out with some non-RGB fans eventually.
