This was my first time trying out turbotax and it seemed like it was doing a good job, but at the end, it told me that I'd have to pay an extra $25 to finish filing my taxes. I was under the impression that "Fed + Efile + State" meant that it would efile both my federal and state taxes. It did give me the option to print and mail my state taxes, but that just seems like a waste of paper. I ended up not using it for the state return and instead filed through my state's website. The whole ordeal feels very much like a bait & switch tactic, and I don't think I'll be buying TurboTax again.
