I ordered 4 of this shelving unit and all 4 arrived damaged. All of the boxes were beat up and re-taped, and nearly all of the MDF shelves were damaged to varying degrees - several were missing chunks bigger than my fist. The MDF boards that were up against the metal parts also had some damage that was clearly caused by the metal parts of the shelf, because they weren't secured at all. There was a bit of plastic and styrofoam, but for the most part, the metal bits could slide around and scrape/gouge the MDF from the inside. The metal parts themselves fared better than the MDF, maybe not pristine but acceptable condition.

I decided to return all 4 units. Despite having it shipped to my house, the Lowe's website made me take it to a store to return it. There's a Lowe's in my town, but website made it look like I had to go to a specific store in a different town, literally 10 times the distance. Fortunately I ignored that and the folks at my local Lowe's accepted the return without any hassle.

Overall this was a big waste of time and a very disappointing experience. IMO Lowe's shouldn't offer shipping on this if they can't deliver it intact. And they should also fix their returns process to suggest the nearest store and allow shipping.

Note: these were labeled as 60 inches wide when I ordered them, but the product page is now for the 48-inch wide version. I believe I received the 60-inch version, although I didnt actually measure them. The url indicates that its for a nightstand, so I think this page has been recycled a couple of times.
