Mostly happy with this keyboard, but I am a little annoyed that they screwed up the USB-C port, so you *have* to use a USB-A to USB-C cable. If you use a USB-C to USB-C cable, it doesn't work at all.

(It could be due to incompetence, but is more likely because they were trying to save a penny per unit by skipping the resistors that are required by the USB-C spec.)

The RGB lights and patterns are pretty, and the keys feel OK to type on. Not very loud (which I appreciate). The keys feel pretty overloaded, but that's to be expected with a 60% keyboard, and the compactness is beneficial.
