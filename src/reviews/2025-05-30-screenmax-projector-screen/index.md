I picked this up when it when it was on sale for $43, and for that price I'm satisfied with it. However I'm dinging it one star because some of the metal rods that make up the frame aren't threaded correctly, so instead of screwing together tightly, they can just slide in and out. This is fine once's it's assembled, because the screen itself holds everything taught. But it makes assembly & disassembly a bit harder because things can fall apart that weren't supposed to.

With my Dangbei N2 projector set about 10 feet away, the picture looked pretty good to me. Definitely better than the wall & ceiling I had been projecting on before!

I'm about 6' tall and I was just barely able to assemble it on my own. The second time around I grabbed a small stool that gave me an extra 6-8" and it was a lot easier.
