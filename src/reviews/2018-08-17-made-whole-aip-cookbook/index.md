As someone who also went through the AIP and still can’t do dairy or nightshades this book feels like the cookbook I always wanted but never knew how much I needed. Wanting to maintain nutrient density and low carb has been challenging but these recipes are tasty and simple. The ease and speed with which this expectant mama and mother of a four year old can get dinner on the table is perfection!
