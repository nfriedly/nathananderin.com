What do you say when a book causes you to actually think through how humans came to be as they are today especially when it comes to the female body because that is exactly what this book did. Having grown up in the rural Christian Conservative Midwest I am deep into the sorting through and throwing out ideas I was raised with and no longer believe. While I’ve have never been a literal 7 day creationist, I don’t think I had actually thought through what it meant to believe the earth is old and we evolved.  I’m still thinking about it and I imagine I will be for a long while.
