Third act break ups can take a long walk off a short pier.  I liked Lucie and Aiden. I loved their slow burn but once the heat came on I was roasting. 🥵 I guess that is my only complaint besides the third act break up. That being said the break up felt important but half hearted which is good because I certainly didn’t want to believe Aiden and Lucie could be that easily broken up.
