I have loved this entire series. This whole series is like a pan of fresh chocolate chip cookies you’ll eat the first one and swear that was enough and then you end up finishing the series because the first one was so good you just couldn’t stop at one and instead had three. 😁

I look forward to reading more by Erin Sterling, and I hope she keeps all the magical cozy vibes.

This story in particular was really enjoyable for me.  It might be good that I can’t remember the other two books all that well except that I enjoyed them.  It has been almost two years since I read the first two books in the series and I can’t really remember Bowen or  Tamsyn from the other books. That does not however mean that I didn’t eat up there love story. I love that their romance started the instant they met but their relationship was definitely built overtime and long distance. I also love how it felt like they gave into the love and affection they already had for one another by the time they have time traveled.

I loved it all so much I hope to read again at some point. This works as a spooky season or holiday season read. Just enjoy it.
