This is probably my favorite book of Carley’s and I’m not entirely sure I would have read it without it fulfilling a reading challenge prompt. I have had it on my TBR since it released, but just hadn’t gotten to it.

I have a challenging relationship with her books. This one felt worth it for the most part. It was emotional but good.
