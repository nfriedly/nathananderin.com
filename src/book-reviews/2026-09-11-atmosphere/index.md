I really loved this story. I’m 100% here for the sapphic romance and the women in the 1980s NASA space exploration.

I cried more than a few times. I found the dual timeline hard because I felt I was actively falling in love with people who are actively in jeopardy in the “actively in space” timeline.

I think the ending here is genius, but I am the kind of person who will also almost always want more. This book is absolutely no exception. I wanted more and was actually a little mad that things ended where they did. It was also genius.
