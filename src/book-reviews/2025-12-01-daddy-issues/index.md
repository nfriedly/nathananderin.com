Kate Goldbeck, GIRL!  She does it again. This book was emotional and 🥵! I don’t know how she does it but her characters feel real and the way she writes in queer representation and neurodivergent characters as well feel pretty true to life. I loved this one. I’m not sure I can say I loved it more than You, Again but about as much would probably be accurate. She has become someone whose books I will read as soon as I can get my hands on them. Keep up the good work Kate. I’m here for it.
