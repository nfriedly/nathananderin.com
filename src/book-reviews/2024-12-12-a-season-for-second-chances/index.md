I’m usually not one for any cheating in a storyline, but I’ll give this one a pass.  It gets a pass because it was the inciting incident that brought about the rest of the story and the rest of the story was really very good and probably made this my second favorite romance by Jenny Bayliss.

I also want a book club like the one found in the pages of this book.
