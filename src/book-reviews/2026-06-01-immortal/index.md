This story and the audiobook performance are top tier. Thank you Sue Lynn Tan and Natalie Naudus! The yearning and world building and intrigue were all very, very good. I throughly enjoyed my listen to this book and want to read/listen to more.

If you are curious about spice level there technically is one scene, but it is very light on details and tastefully descriptive. It felt very appropriate to the story.
