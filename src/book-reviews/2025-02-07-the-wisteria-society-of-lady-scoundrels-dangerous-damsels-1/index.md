If you have to have it explained to you why there must be flying houses and lady pirates 🏴‍☠️ then this is not the book or series for you.  If however that very idea delights your heart and soul please look no farther. I throughly enjoyed this read. It was a joyous, delightful little romp that did not stress me out.
