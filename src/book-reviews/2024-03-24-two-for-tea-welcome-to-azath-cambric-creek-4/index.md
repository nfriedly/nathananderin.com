I’ve throughly enjoyed C. M. Nascosta’s other books and I cannot wait for the Girls Weekend books coming later this year. That being said, this book had so much potential that I just don’t think was realized. I wanted so much more.  It felt like a solid start to something and then it just ended.

However as a fan of Girls Weekend and the author I feel pretty confident that the books to follow will be help bring the story around. I’m hopeful.
