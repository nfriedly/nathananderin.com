Quick, fun, with a little spice, pretty much exactly what I was expecting. Not sure I found these high school rivals to lovers all that believable, but I’m also not a huge fan of the trope for that reason. It feels too close to, “He picks on you/bullies you so he must like you,” and I am not a fan.
