My daughter gave this 5 stars and my son gave it 4. I liked this sequel better than book one. In many ways the first book is fine but in the second book Peter and Pax come full circle with sacrifice and hardship and it’s beautiful.

Peter has sustained more hardship at the started of this book which is heartbreaking but by the end of this book you have seen him go on journey and come home to himself and who he is and what he wants for himself.
