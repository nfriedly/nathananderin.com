Maybe my second favorite Katherine Center book. I’m a total sucker for the friends to lovers trope. That said even in it’s silliness and predictability I enjoyed the story. This was fun and not gut wrenching or too much much a roller coaster. I throughly enjoyed it and it was fun. Also closed door. 🚪
