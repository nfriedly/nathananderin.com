I’m well aware that is book is accurately described as raunch-com but I am 100% here for it. I actually enjoyed this WAY more than the other books by this same author duo, but if you are not ready for all the smut and spice DO NOT READ this book! Move along, this is not for you.

I enjoyed the smut. I enjoyed the plot. I loved the FMC and the MMC and I want more books in this series yesterday. Julie and Sierra are only getting better as they work together more.
