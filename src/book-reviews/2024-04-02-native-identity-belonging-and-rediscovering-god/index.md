It is the work and writing of women like Kaitlin that has encouraged me to find a way forward in life and in faith even if it doesn’t look like something I grew up knowing or understanding. Reading her words makes me want to reach for ever more inclusive faith that can face the lament of our history and our present so that the world we build will be interested and based on a foundation of listening, watching, and doing the work.
