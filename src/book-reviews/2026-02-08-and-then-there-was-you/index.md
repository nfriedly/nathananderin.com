I liked this, I think. 🤔 I know I wanted to like this but I kept getting hung up on the sci-fi plot twist I did not see coming. Rob as a character brought on the uncanny valley effect for me at least. I mean the idea is worth exploring but it was wicked off putting for me in this day and age.

I found Chloe and her struggles very believable. I also loved what was blossoming between her and John and the whole thing with Sean felt a little daunting too.
