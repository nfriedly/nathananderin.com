This is probably my second favorite book in the Legends & Latte series. My favorite being Bookshops and Bonedust. Travis Baldree seems to strike the very nearly perfect balance between cozy fantasy and adventure at least for my heart and tastes anyway.  I found myself not wanting this story to end and wanting it to last a bit longer all the time. I throughly enjoyed the way the story progressed and even the way it eventually ended. I will be eagerly awaiting the next story in this series. May it come quickly.

Also stand ovation for the author’s outstanding performance of his own writing for the audiobook version! 👏🏻👏🏻👏🏻👏🏻
