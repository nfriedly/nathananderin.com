I’m a straight up sucker for this book and all books like it. I will love it with everything in me for always. I like the silly playful parts, the adventure, the fantastic, and of course the happy ending. I appreciate a lot of different kinds of books, but this is exactly what I am looking for and longing for most of the time. What an absolutely delightful adventure fantasy romance!

I will also say I enjoyed that the level of spice and felt that it fit the characters and the story without getting in the way or overtaking the plot.
