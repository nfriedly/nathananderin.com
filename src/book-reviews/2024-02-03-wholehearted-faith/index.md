I just finished listening to the audio version and I wish I had listened sooner. What a beautiful way to remember and hear the words of a woman who sought to live and speak like Jesus.

Tears sprung to my eyes immediately upon hearing Rachel’s words in the voices of her family and friends.
Rachel Held Evans has always had a way of putting things that resonated with me. Without women like Rachel I would have probably walked away from my faith long ago.
