We are huge fans of Roz and were so excited for this third book especially after we loved the first two books SO much.  We liked this book but I wouldn’t say we loved it.  The story is compelling and we see lots of development in Roz’s character and life. If you love Roz and Brightbill you will want to read on but this book doesn’t seem to captivate the same way the first two do.
