This is such a beautiful and articulate book. I am already looking forward to rereading it. It was probably one of the best books I could have chosen to read after the U.S. presidential election of 2024.

While many part of the book are memorable and worth the reread Chapter 12 on toxic masculinity will stand out in my mind as some of the best writing I have read on the subject.

As someone who has also struggled with depression this feels like an empathic love letter to seek out healing as much as you can for as long as you can with the help of those you love and who love you.
