I’m torn between 3 and 4 stars but rounded up because it want to see more books like this. I loved the adventure and the fantasy world but found the romance and smut underwhelming. 🤷🏻‍♀️ I was not invested in the romance. I liked Cinnamon and want a skirt with pockets full of everything for myself.  I will keep reading the series because it’s fun and makes me smile.  I liked the characters and the adventure and the world it all takes place in but the romance/smut left me wanting to skip ahead.
