This is fine. I read the entire series. I’m not sure I found much in this book or series memorable. Like there is love and spice and it’s cute and all, and nothing stood out as bad.

I guess the more I read the more I know when I love and adore something and as I said to begin with this was fine but that’s all. I’m not on fire to share. I’ll just give it a pass and move along.

Okay, after really thinking about it the author’s portrayal of a college aged woman of a certain reputation wanting a change and feeling kind of trapped by her old rep when she has grown and changed felt well done and relatable. Also Kian is a total golden retriever.
