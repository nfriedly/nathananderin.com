I’m a sucker for Abby Jimenez.

I love the main characters and the way she writes to make place a part of the story.  I kept wondering how this was the third book in the series and then I knew. Wowza that was a lot of drama before the end of the book.

I’m a sucker for love that shows up and is vulnerable and brave. I’m a little tired of unrealistic grand gestures and third act break ups that don’t really have to happen.
