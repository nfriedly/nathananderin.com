An excellent family read aloud.

I’m not sure what I was expecting. This book had been recommended to me as a family read aloud by a few people and I’m glad I finally read it/listened to it. My children loved it and so did I. It had my heart the whole way through. The moral and meaning of the story is important and rings so true.
