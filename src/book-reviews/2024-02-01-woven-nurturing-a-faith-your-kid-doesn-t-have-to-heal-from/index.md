This book is one I wish would have existed when I started my parenting journey.  As I’ve continued listening to the audiobook, I have been consistently recommending it to quite a few friends who have kids and feel uncertain at best about how to share faith with their children because they are still figuring out what faith looks like and the faith they were raised in held no space for wondering, curiosity, and uncertainty.

This book is very practical in sharing ways to talk about and think about Bible stories that encourages kids to discover their own faith along side their caregivers.
