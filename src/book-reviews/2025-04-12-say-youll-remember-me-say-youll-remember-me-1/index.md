I am wrecked and I was so invested!  I love Abby Jimenez! I related to Samantha. Caring for a loved one is hard no matter what the illness or how long it lasts. I’ve also done long distance and it is not for the weak of heart. I was rooting for these two the whole time and even though this book was one I could not put down and I was an emotional mess through the entire read (because of all they went through) I’m glad someone finally talked some sense into Xavier.
