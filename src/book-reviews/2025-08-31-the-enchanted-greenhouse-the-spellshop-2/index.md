There is something to be said for books that let you feel like you can put them down and come back to them and read them at your own pace. This book was like a warm blanket or a perfect cup of tea.  It made me feel no rush to finish or keep going when I needed to sleep, and I understand that isn’t what everyone is looking for but after reading some heavier books earlier this month this was a welcome reprieve.
