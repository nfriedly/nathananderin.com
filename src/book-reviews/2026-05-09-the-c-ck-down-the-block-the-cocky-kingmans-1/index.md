Okay. I loved this. It is absolutely fluffy smut but it is fluffy smut I throughly enjoyed listening to. I’m not sure I would have enjoyed actually reading the text nearly as much as the audiobook. This audiobook was exactly what I needed after reading some much heavier nonfiction recently. This book wasn’t written to become a National Book Award Finalist or Pulitzer Prize winner but damn if I didn’t enjoy it enough to move straight into the next book in the series.

Also extra points for the MMC being absolutely gone for the FMC from page one and for no third act break up because seriously no one needs that shit.
