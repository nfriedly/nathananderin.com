The first three books in this series were wonderful and were an absolute delight to read with my kids this one however wrecked me in the best possible way. This book is a gentle love letter to all those who have experienced deep grief that mandated it take up the space and be felt.  I can’t say more. 5 stars every time, all day long, cried multiple times, have 0 regrets. Favorite book of the series so far.
