I enjoy Emily’s writing style.  It feels very relatable and usually resonates as true to my lived experience and she gives you good health ways to survive/cope with burnout which is a very real problem for some of us. As a neurodivergent woman, I usually struggle very hard to feel as though I belong anywhere. This book helped me feel a sense of solidarity and support with Emily and other women in general.
