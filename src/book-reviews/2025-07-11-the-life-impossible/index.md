This was an interesting read. I honestly wasn’t at all sure what to expect when I checked this book out. The narrator did an excellent job in their delivery of the story.  Through out most of the book I wasn’t entirely sure what genre of fiction I was reading. Parts of the book felt very much like a mystery while others had more science fiction vibes.

I enjoyed and deeply resonated with the books take on second chances and living life to the fullest. I also enjoyed the authors thoughts and ideas about the interconnection of all life on earth. That being said ai can see how this book isn’t for everybody. Through out most of the first half of the book I wasn’t at all sure what I was reading and felt a bit lost. I think I’m glad I stuck it out. The audiobook made getting through it much easier.
