In the beginning of this book I was laughing out loud and intrigued by all the possibilities, but the book didn’t deliver the goods for me. I found the relationship between Nina and Tom to be lack luster, I wasn’t sure I wanted anything for them.  I liked parts of the ended, but I got lost in the details and secondary character. Not my cup of tea.  Was hoping for a lot my laughs.
