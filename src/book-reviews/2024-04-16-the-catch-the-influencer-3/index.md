I liked Set On You and I loved Exes and O’s. This was my least favorite book of the series.  I wasn’t rooting for the characters or their love story.  I thought the characters and their development was lack luster and how they got together contrived at best.  This does feel very similar to It Happened One Summer by Tessa Bailey.  Other reviews called it a mash up of While You Were Sleeping and The Proposal and I love those movies. While I understand the references, this book did not live up to that hype.  It was fine. I finished it,  but I was disappointed.  My expectations were high and did not get met.
