I’m giving this five stars because why wouldn’t I? It was exactly my cup of tea. Exactly what I needed.

I’m giving it 5 stars knowing full well there are people for whom this will not be a five start read and that is okay.

I’m not usually one for zombies and the only other exception was Santa Clarita Diet. I love vampires and have been a fan since I started watching Buffy in my teenage years.  I have read a few of the other vampire romances out there and they were fine but I should have known Olivia Dade was going to knock the ball out of the park.

Love her and loved the audiobook version the only thing that would have made the audiobook better was Joy Nash actually singing the Gaston Song. IYKYK
