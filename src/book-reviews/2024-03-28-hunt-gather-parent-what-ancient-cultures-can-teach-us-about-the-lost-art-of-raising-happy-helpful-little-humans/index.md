I enjoyed this book and have recommended it to others. I think most of  the principles and practical examples are sound, but as a mom of two kids (10 year old boy and 5 year old girl at the time of reading) this felt very geared towards young kids and families with only one kid and in general from only the perspective of a mom of a single daughter who is very young.
