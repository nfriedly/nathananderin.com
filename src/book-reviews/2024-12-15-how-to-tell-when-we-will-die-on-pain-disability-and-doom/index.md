I am not sure what I was expecting, but this is a book I want to sit with and think about and think over. It took me over 10 years to come to grips with my own chronic illness/disability/autoimmune disease whatever the hell you want to call it or name it.

So much of this book smacks and just keeps hitting you. I think the author would be satisfied to hear that.
