If you’re looking for love and adventure with some magic thrown in, this book is probably for you. This book felt slow for all the action that was going on especially compared to the author’s other series Dangerous Damsels.

I will keep reading/listening to this authors books as I generally find their books a delight, but as I said earlier this one’s pace felt drawn out and slower and led to me enjoying this particular story only a little bit less than the others I have read.
