“Think: Gilmore Girls, but with murder.”

That’s a pretty good summary. Those are definitely the vibes. Dysfunctional family dynamics and a want to figure out who committed the murder next door make for a pretty interesting read.

I won’t be chasing you down to put this book in your hands, but it’s worth your time if you are looking for a good mystery with while relationship development between grandmother, mother, and daughter.
