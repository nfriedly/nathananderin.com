---
title: "The Color of Night"
author: nathan
date: "2026-07-26"
---

It’s the color of rest, of sleep, of rejuvenation.
Not death, not stopped, just a slowing down.
Reflecting.
The color of a long day come to an end.
The color of a new beginning starting.
It washes over you and flows around you.
It calms and enchants, or excites and surprises.
It’s the color of teleportation and mixed up stories.
Of sparkle, shadow, and dreams.
